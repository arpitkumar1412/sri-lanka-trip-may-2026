# Sri Lanka Trip — Final Itinerary & Booking Pack

**Group of 6 · Hyderabad → Colombo → Arugam Bay · 28 May – 2 June 2026**

---

## Why East Coast (and not the South)?

Late May is the start of Sri Lanka's southwest (Yala) monsoon — the west and south coasts (Colombo, Galle, Mirissa, Hikkaduwa, Bentota) get heavy rain and rough seas. The east coast is in its dry season, and Arugam Bay kicks off its peak surf + party season the last week of May.

**Verdict:** Skip the south/west beaches. Head east to Arugam Bay.

## 1. Visa — Sri Lanka ETA (Free for Indians)

- **Cost:** Free (30-day double-entry tourist visa)
- **Apply at:** [eta.gov.lk (official portal)](https://www.eta.gov.lk)
- **Reference guide:** [BusinessToday — Sri Lanka visa-free for Indians 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/nri/visa/story/sri-lanka-visa-free-for-indians-in-2026-how-to-get-your-free-eta-what-to-carry-all-you-need-to-know-525971-2026-04-17)
- **Steps:** Tourist ETA → Individual application → enter passport + travel dates + Colombo hotel address → submit (no doc upload, no fee). Approval in 24–48 hrs by email. All 6 apply individually around 18–20 May.
- **Avoid:** Third-party "visa service" sites. They charge fees for what's free.

## 3. Internal Transfer — Colombo ↔ Arugam Bay

For 6 people, **private AC van beats internal flights** ($200–280 vs ~$900 for 6 air seats).

- **Hire:** [PickMe (Sri Lanka's Uber, has long-distance vans)](https://pickme.lk)

**Backup (if energy is low on Day 5):** [FitsAir BTC→CMB](https://www.fitsair.com) or [Cinnamon Air](https://www.cinnamonair.com) for the return only.

## 5. Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1 — Thu 28 May · Arrive Colombo

**Transit:** SriLankan UL HYD→CMB (10:15 dep · ~12:30 land). Pick up [Dialog SIM](https://www.dialog.lk) at airport. 1 hr taxi to Colombo 03.

**Afternoon (2:00–6:00 PM):**

- Lunch at **Ministry of Crab** (Sri Lanka's most famous restaurant) — [ministryofcrab.com](https://www.ministryofcrab.com)
- Walk **Galle Face Green** promenade — sunset spot
- Quick stop at **Gangaramaya Temple**

**Evening (6:00 PM onwards):**

- Sundowner at **On14 Rooftop Bar** (OZO Hotel, Colombo) — [On14](https://www.on14.lk)
- Dinner at **Nuga Gama** (Cinnamon Grand) for traditional Sri Lankan, or **Upali's by Nawaloka** for casual local
- Late drinks: **Cloud Café (Hilton)** or **B52** if up for clubbing — [B52 Colombo](https://www.b52colombo.com)

**Stay:** [Luxury Ocean-View Apartment Colombo 03](https://www.airbnb.com/colombo-03-sri-lanka/stays/apartments) (or backup [Cinnamon Red](https://www.booking.com))

---

### Day 2 — Fri 29 May · Colombo → Arugam Bay (Travel Day)

**Transit:** Private van pickup at 5:30 AM. Drive Colombo → A-Bay via Ratnapura/Monaragala. Arrive ~3 PM.

- Stops en route: breakfast at Kandy bypass, lunch at **Sri Latha Restaurant** (Monaragala)
- Book van: [PickMe](https://pickme.lk) or via your Colombo hotel concierge

**Late afternoon (4:00 PM):**

- Check in, beach walk to **Main Point** surf break
- Sunset beers at **Mambo's Beach Bar** — Friday night = the scene begins. [Mambo's Arugam Bay](https://www.facebook.com/MamboArugamBay/)

**Evening:**

- Dinner at **Hello Burger** or **Sooriya Beach Hut**
- Easy night — saving energy for Saturday

**Stay:** [Beach Escape Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/659327237503070174) or [Croco Beach House Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/25094803)

---

### Day 3 — Sat 30 May · Arugam Bay (Surf + Big Night)

**Morning (8–11 AM):**

- Breakfast at **Hello Burger** or villa-cooked
- Surf lesson at **Baby Point** (~$15/lesson, beginners) or **Main Point** (intermediate). Rentals everywhere on the strip.

**Afternoon (12–5 PM):**

- Lunch at **Stardust Beach Hotel** (best Sri Lankan + seafood)
- Lazy beach + reading
- Optional: bike to **Whiskey Point** for afternoon surf

**Evening (6 PM onwards):**

- Pre-game at **Whiskey Point** sunset bar
- Dinner at **Gecko Restaurant** or **Stardust**
- **Mambo's Saturday party** — biggest night of the week, DJs on the sand till sunrise. THE event of the trip.

**Stay:** Same as Day 2

---

### Day 4 — Sun 31 May · A-Bay Side Trips + Recovery

**Morning (7–10 AM):**

- **Pottuvil Lagoon Safari** — spot elephants, crocodiles, mangroves. ~$21/pp, 2 hrs. Book: [Arugam Bay Agenda](https://www.arugambayagenda.com/wildlifetours/lagoon-safari-pottuvil/) or [Arugam Bay Safari](https://arugambaysafari.com/tour/lagoon-safari-pottuvil/) or [GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/pottuvil-l158774/pottuvil-lagoon-eco-tour-mangrove-adventure-in-arugam-bay-t510041/)

**Midday (11 AM–3 PM):**

- Drive 12 min south to **Elephant Rock** — climb for the view, swim at the secluded beach below
- Lunch at **Peanut Farm beach** shacks (15 min south, quieter than A-Bay strip) or **Pasarichenai Beach**

**Afternoon (3–6 PM):**

- Optional: **Kudumbigala Monastery** (ancient hilltop monastery, 30 min south) or **Muhudu Maha Viharaya** ruins in Pottuvil
- Or just chill at the villa pool

**Evening:**

- Sundowners at **Upali's** (Sunday recovery vibe — chill, sandy)
- Dinner at the villa or **Siam View Hotel** (pizza + cheap beer)
- Early night

**Stay:** Same as Day 2

---

### Day 5 — Mon 1 Jun · A-Bay → Colombo (Travel Back)

**Morning:**

- Sunrise walk on the beach
- Breakfast at villa, last dip
- Van pickup ~7 AM

**Transit options:**

- **A.** Private van A-Bay → CMB direct (8–9 hrs) — book via villa
- **B.** Faster: 1.5 hr van A-Bay → Batticaloa, then [FitsAir BTC→CMB](https://www.fitsair.com) ~1 hr

**Late afternoon (arrive Colombo ~4–5 PM):**

- Check in to Colombo apartment, freshen up
- Last-minute shopping at **Odel** (Sri Lankan fashion + souvenirs) or **Barefoot** (textiles)

**Evening:**

- Farewell dinner at **Ministry of Crab** ([book ahead](https://www.ministryofcrab.com)) — saved for the last night
- Final sundowner at **On14 Rooftop Bar** or **Cloud Café (Hilton)**
- Easy night — early flight tomorrow

**Stay:** Colombo apartment (return-night booking) — see [Section 4](#4-stays)

---

### Day 6 — Tue 2 Jun · Colombo → HYD (Departure)

**Morning:**

- Breakfast near the apartment (Galle Face area: **Café Kumbuk** or hotel café)
- Quick stop for any pending souvenirs / cash exchange at the mall
- Taxi to **CMB airport** — leave 3.5–4 hrs before flight (Colombo traffic + immigration queues)

**Afternoon/Evening:** IndiGo / SriLankan CMB→HYD return (whichever direct slot you booked). Land HYD same evening.
